Sentry Alert — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who this is

Sentry Alert ("the app", called Sentry on your home screen) is developed and published by Fabian E. Pacheco-Ferreira, a U.S. Government employee, for AFCENT, under AFCENT's authority ("we"). You can reach us at fabian.pacheco-ferreira@us.af.mil.

The app is for a closed group. It does nothing until you join a location with a QR code and passcode given to you in person. It is not available to the public and it does not replace emergency services.

What the app does

A location's administrators raise an alarm level, and the app notifies everyone enrolled at that location. To do that, it has to know which phones belong to which location and how to reach them.

What we do not collect

Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or microphone. No advertising identifier. No analytics or crash-reporting service. No tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not use your location except for a help request you choose to send. The camera is used only to read a QR code on your phone; the picture is not saved or sent anywhere.

Who can see it

  • Administrators of the location you joined, who see a roster of the phones enrolled there and the information in the table above that relates to their location. They do not see your DoD ID in full.
  • People who run the service: the developer, Fabian E. Pacheco-Ferreira, and authorised staff of AFCENT. They can reach the stored data in order to operate and secure it.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who processes it

Two providers handle data for us, and there are no others:

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Functions, Firestore, Cloud KMS, Secret Manager, Firebase Hosting and Cloud Messaging) stores and serves the data. The database is in the United States (a Google multi-region, nam5) and the server functions run in us-central1. The encryption key that protects the DoD ID is held in Google's Tel Aviv region (me-west1), so the server calls that region to read the number.
  • Apple delivers push notifications through its push service. On current builds the alarm text is sealed with a key held on your phone before it is sent, so Apple's push service carries it without reading it.

Both receive the data needed to do that job and are bound by their own terms.

Notices shown inside the app

Before you enrol, the app shows a Notice and Consent screen and a Privacy Act Statement. They describe the terms under which AFCENT handles the information you give it.

This policy explains how the app handles your information. It is meant to agree with the Notice and Consent and Privacy Act Statement shown in the app before you enrol. If they differ, the Notice and Consent and Privacy Act Statement control.

The 90-day figure is a setting we can change, and it applies only to devices that leave after the change.

Your choices

  • Leave a location from the top-left menu in the app. Alarms stop, your DoD ID is erased at once, and the rest of your device record is deleted 90 days later.
  • Turn off permissions in iOS Settings. Without notifications and Critical Alerts the app cannot do its job, but nothing else depends on them.

Deleting your information

Open the app and choose Leave Location. Your DoD ID is erased at once and the rest of your device record is deleted 90 days later.

If you can no longer use the phone (for example, it was lost, wiped or uninstalled), email fabian.pacheco-ferreira@us.af.mil with your location and the approximate date you enrolled. We will confirm the request with your location's administrator and, once confirmed, remove the device record within 30 days and reply to tell you it is done.

Some records cannot be removed on request: records of administrator actions are kept in an archive and are not edited or deleted on request.

Security

The DoD ID is encrypted with a managed key service before it is stored. Each phone signs its requests with a key that never leaves it. Connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise otherwise.

Children

The app is for adults enrolled at a workplace location. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We will change the "Last updated" date at the top when this policy changes, and post the new version at this address.
